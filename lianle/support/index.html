首页 隐私政策

数据保存在哪里？

练了以当前设备的本地数据库为准。完成的训练、体重、习惯、动作库和偏好可通过你的 iCloud 私有数据库同步到登录同一 Apple 账户的设备；无需创建练了账号。进行中的训练和休息计时只保存在当前设备。

如何备份数据？

打开“设置”，选择“导出数据 (CSV)”，再通过 iOS 文件选择器保存到你选择的位置。

如何删除数据？

打开“设置 > 数据与同步”，选择“清除全部数据”并完成二次确认。本机数据会立即删除，当前 iCloud 账户中的练了数据会在联网后删除。仅卸载 App 只删除本机副本，已同步数据可能在重装后恢复。删除前建议先导出需要保留的记录。

为什么没有同步？

请在“设置 > 数据与同步”查看 iCloud 状态，并确认设备已登录 iCloud 且网络可用。系统可能根据电量和网络延后后台同步；本地记录不受影响。若更换了 iCloud 账户，为防止数据混合，练了会暂停同步；请切回原账户，或在确认不需要本机数据后执行“清除全部数据”再采用当前账户。

计时或记录异常怎么办？

请先完全退出并重新打开 App。如果问题仍然存在，请在邮件中说明 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、App 版本、操作步骤和你看到的结果。

Help & Support (English)

Where is my data stored?

Lianle uses the local database on your device. Completed workouts, weight, habits, exercise-library data, and preferences can sync to devices signed in to the same Apple Account through your iCloud private database. No Lianle account is required. Active workouts and rest timers stay on the current device.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings > Data & sync and choose Export data (CSV). Use the iOS file picker to save the file where you choose.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings > Data & sync, choose Clear all data, and confirm. Local data is deleted immediately. Lianle data in your current iCloud account is deleted when the device is online. Uninstalling the app deletes only the local copy; synced data may return after reinstalling. Export any records you want to keep before deleting.

Why is my data not syncing?

In Settings > Data & sync, check the iCloud status and make sure the device is signed in to iCloud and connected to the internet. The system may delay background sync based on network and battery conditions; local records remain available. If you changed iCloud accounts, Lianle pauses sync to prevent mixing data. Switch back to the original account, or clear all data before adopting the new account.

What should I do if a timer or record looks wrong?

Quit and reopen the app. If the issue continues, email your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce, and what you saw.
